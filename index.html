INDEPENDENT BRAND IDENTITY PROPOSAL

A crest for the next chapter.

A proposal to preserve Coventry City FC’s civic heritage while rebuilding its crest into a clearer, more scalable identity system for contemporary football culture.

Coventry City Football Club Identity Renewal Proposal Park Jaeyoung · Seoul 2026

01 / THE MOMENT

25 YEARS AWAY. ONE NEW BEGINNING.

Back where you belong.

The 2026/27 season marks Coventry City’s return to the Premier League after a 25-year absence. A sporting return of this scale is more than a change of division — it is a moment when the club is once again seen by a truly global audience.

A modern football identity now has to live everywhere at once. The crest becomes a shirt badge, a broadcast mark, a social avatar, an app icon, a stadium sign, a retail object and a supporter symbol. That shift creates a simple design question: can Coventry become clearer without becoming less Coventry?

02 / WHY PROPOSE CHANGE?

Heritage is powerful.
Complexity is not the same thing.

The existing Coventry City crest is built on a rich symbolic foundation. Its elephant, castle, eagle, phoenix, flame, football and club name carry history, pride and resilience.

The problem is not a lack of meaning. The challenge is how all of that meaning performs in contemporary brand environments. At smaller scales, detailed illustration and a broad colour range can lose clarity. The proposal therefore does not aim to remove heritage, but to reorganise it into a clearer hierarchy with stronger reproduction, recognition and consistency.

03 / KEEP THE MEMORY

Do not replace history.
Reframe it.

The renewal is based on inheritance rather than replacement. Each symbol remains because each one carries a different part of Coventry’s civic and football identity.

The redesign changes drawing, proportion, line weight and hierarchy — not the story itself. The objective is to make those inherited symbols easier to recognise and easier to use without stripping away the identity supporters already know.

04 / CREST RENEWAL

One circle.
A clearer order.

The renewed design transforms the illustrative heraldic composition into a structured circular football badge.

A bold navy outer ring creates a stable silhouette and integrates the club name into one clear lock-up. Inside, the elephant and three towers remain the primary heritage anchor, while the eagle, phoenix, football and flame are reorganised into a more controlled visual hierarchy. The difference between before and after is therefore not the deletion of history, but the creation of order.

05 / COLOR SYSTEM

Sky Blue is not decoration.
It is recognition.

The renewed palette clarifies which colours lead and which colours support.

Sky Blue becomes the emotional signature. Navy gives structure and authority. White creates clarity and breathing room. Red is reserved for selected symbolic accents so that it adds energy without competing with the core identity.

06 / APPLICATION SYSTEM

Not just a badge.
A club-wide identity tool.

The renewed crest is intended as a practical brand asset, not a logo that exists only on a presentation board.

It needs to move naturally from the chest of a shirt to supporter flags, digital content, stadium graphics, vehicles, merchandise and printed materials while remaining unmistakably the same identity. The studies below test the proposal across scale, material and context.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE
