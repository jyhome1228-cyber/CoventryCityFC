01 — THE MOMENT

25 years away. One new beginning.

Back where
you belong.

The 2026/27 season marks Coventry City's return to the Premier League after a 25-year absence. A sporting return of this scale is more than a change of division — it is a rare moment when the club is seen again by a truly global audience.

Shirts become international objects. The crest becomes an app icon, a broadcast mark, a social avatar, a stadium sign and a piece of supporter culture — often within the same day. This proposal asks one simple question: can Coventry's identity become clearer without becoming less Coventry?

02 — WHY PROPOSE CHANGE?

Heritage is powerful.
Complexity is not the same thing.

Coventry City's crest is rich with civic memory and meaning. But as identities move from embroidered shirts to tiny screens, broadcast graphics, merchandise, stadium systems and social media, dense detail can lose clarity. The answer is not to erase meaning. It is to give meaning a stronger hierarchy.

THE CHALLENGE

Too much information
at the smallest scale.

  • Dense illustration
  • Multiple competing symbols

03 — KEEP THE MEMORY

Do not replace history.
Reframe it.

The proposal keeps the symbolic language that makes the club inseparable from Coventry itself. The change is in drawing, proportion and hierarchy — not in abandoning the story.

01

Elephant

A direct civic reference and one of the most recognisable anchors in Coventry's visual history.

02

Three Towers

A silhouette tied to the city skyline and a compact signal of place.

03

Eagle

A heritage element retained with simplified geometry and clearer balance.

04

Phoenix

A symbol of Coventry reborn — especially resonant in a club entering another era of renewal.

05

Flame

Reduced to a controlled red accent: energy, recovery and forward movement.

06

Football

The simplest truth at the centre of every application: this is a football club first.

PRESERVE → SIMPLIFY → STRENGTHEN
04 — CREST ARCHITECTURE

One circle.
A clearer order.

The proposed badge is organised as a modern circular system: a bold navy ring, a confident club name, simplified civic symbols at the centre and a small, controlled use of red. The silhouette becomes more stable and more recognisable before the details are even read.

05 — DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Built to feel familiar
before it feels new.

01

Recognisable

The outer silhouette and core symbols should read instantly — even at broadcast or social-avatar size.

02

Scalable

One badge needs to survive a shirt patch, favicon, scoreboard, flag, vehicle and stadium façade without becoming a different logo each time.

03

Flexible

Full-colour, one-colour and reversed versions should all feel intentional, not compromised.

04

Coventry

The result should never look like a generic modern football badge. Civic memory remains the reason the system exists.

06 — VISUAL SYSTEM

Sky Blue is not decoration.
It is recognition.

The palette is deliberately reduced. Sky blue remains the emotional signature. Navy gives authority and contrast. White creates breathing room. Red appears only where the heritage narrative needs an accent.
